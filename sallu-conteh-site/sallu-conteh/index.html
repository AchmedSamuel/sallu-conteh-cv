01About

Engineer, problem-solver and teacher

I am an electrical and electronic engineer from Sierra Leone. I hold a BSc in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Fourah Bay College, University of Sierra Leone, and have undertaken an MSc in Renewable Energy Study and the Environment.

My industrial attachment at MANTRAC (Caterpillar) SL Limited gave me hands-on experience with preventive maintenance, fault finding on low- and high-voltage networks, and the Caterpillar generator sets at the Kingtom main power station that supply electricity to the Western Area. I have also lectured engineering and business mathematics at IAMTECH College.

I hold a First Division Certificate in Computer Software and work confidently with Microsoft Office, MATLAB (including fuzzy logic), Visual Basic .NET and SPSS.
